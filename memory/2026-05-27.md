**CRITICAL ISSUE:** `SPY_Overnight` in daily_with_index_factors.csv is future data — we don't know SPY's overnight return at 16:00 close.

**Initial (WRONG) conclusion:**
- SPY Up overnight → 62.9% WR
- SPY Strong Up (>0.5%) → 70.7% WR
- SPY Down → 35.4% WR

**FIXED with SPY_DailyChange (known at close):**
- SPY Daily < -2% → 77.16% WR (mean reversion! when SPY drops big during day, stocks bounce overnight)
- SPY Daily < -1% → 63.46% WR
- SPY Daily < -0.5% → 61.12% WR
- SPY Daily > +1% → 31.56% WR (avoid when SPY rallies hard — profit taking overnight)

**Interpretation:** This is mean reversion. When SPY drops during the day, stocks are oversold and bounce back overnight. When SPY rallies, profit-taking pushes stocks down overnight.
