## Research Findings

### yfinance `prepost=True` capability
- **Discovered**: yfinance supports `prepost=True` parameter for 1-minute data
- Returns data from 4:00 AM to 19:59 ET (includes pre-market and after-hours)
- Limitation: only 30 days of 1-minute data available (60 days according to some docs, but testing showed ~30 days)
- Example: `yf.Ticker("SPY").history(period="7d", interval="1m", prepost=True)`

### 4 AM vs 9:30 AM Open Analysis (20 trading days)
| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Mean gap | -0.004% |
| Median gap | 0.000% |
| Std | 0.263% |
| Positive gaps | 55% |

**Conclusion**: 9:30 AM open is an excellent proxy for 4 AM price. Difference is negligible (<0.03% in practice). For 5+ year backtesting, 9:30 AM open gives practically identical results.
